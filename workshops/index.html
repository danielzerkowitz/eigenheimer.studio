N° 01

Who this is for

this is for you

  • You lead a team, a department or a management committee, and the same topic keeps coming back without landing.
  • Your team agrees in the meeting and disagrees in the corridor.
  • You’ve tried to run the session yourself and ended up chairing instead of taking part.
  • You need everyone in the room to own the outcome, not just to have heard it.
  • You have a date and a room. You need someone to make the day count.

this isn’t for.

  • Teambuilding for its own sake. No ropes, no cooking class.
  • A session without a purpose. If nobody can say what has to come out of the day, we’ll look for it in the intake, and if it isn’t there we’ll say so.
  • Training. That’s AI Basics or a programme, not a workshop.
  • Anyone who wants the facilitator to deliver the conclusion.
N° 02

What you get

    Five formats. One rule: you leave with it. One price per session, not per head.

  1. N° 01

    Alignment session

    A team pulling in different directions gets to one picture of what matters, why, and what that means for each of them. Said out loud, written down.

    half day
  2. N° 02

    OKRs and goal-setting

    Objectives and key results set by the people who have to hit them, not handed down. Fewer than you’d like, sharper than you’re used to.

    half or full day
  3. N° 03

    Strategy or team day

    Direction for the year, the plan behind it, and the three things the team stops doing.

    full day
  4. N° 04

    Retrospective

    A project, a quarter, a launch. What happened, what it cost, what changes. Run so people say the true thing, not the safe one.

    half day
  5. N° 05

    Decision workshop

    One knot: a priority, a scope, a go/no-go. Everyone leaves knowing what was decided, who owns it and what happens on Monday.

    half day

Every format ends the same way: a one-page write-up within two working days (decisions, owners, dates, open questions) and a thirty-minute follow-up three weeks later.

N° 03

How it works

  1. N° 01

    Intake

    Thirty minutes. What has to come out of the day, who’s in the room, and what “done” looks like at five o’clock. Quote within two working days.

  2. N° 02

    Design

    An agenda built for that outcome, not a template with your logo on it. You see it and approve it before the day. We speak to two or three participants beforehand.

  3. N° 03

    Facilitate.

    We run the room. You take part. Half a day or a full day, on your premises or wherever you’ve booked.

  4. N° 04

    Write up

    One page within two working days: decisions, owners, dates, what’s still open.

  5. N° 05

    Follow up

    Three weeks later, thirty minutes. What held, what didn’t, what needs another push.

N° 04

Why this facilitator

N° 01

No stake in the answer.

The value of an outsider in the room is that they don’t need the decision to go any particular way. We hold the process. You own the outcome.

N° 02

Built for the outcome. not the format.

The agenda is designed for what has to come out of the day, and you approve it beforehand. On the day, if the room finds the real question is a different one, we follow the room, not the agenda.

N° 03

We’ve sat on your side of the table.

Daniel Zerkowitz facilitates every session himself. Years inside large organisations, running exactly these sessions as the person who had to live with the result afterwards.

N° 05

Questions, before the call

What does it cost? +

Scoped after the intake. On the call we’ll tell you whether it’s a half day or a full day and what that costs, excl. btw. One price per session, not per head. Quote within two working days.

How many people can be in the room? +

Six to sixteen. Above that, a room stops working and starts watching.

Do you facilitate in Dutch or French? +

Dutch or English, whichever the room speaks. French-speaking teams are served in English.

Where does it take place? +

Your premises, or a location you book. Anywhere in Belgium.

How much preparation is there on our side? +

The intake call, a short conversation with two or three participants, and approving the agenda. About two hours in total, spread over the two weeks before.

let’s talk

A 30-minute call. Tell us what has to come out of the day and who’s in the room. We’ll tell you whether a session will get you there, and what it costs.
