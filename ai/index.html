Why this studio

Adoption is overheard. not announced.

Nobody adopts a tool because the CEO’s video said so. They adopt it when a colleague they rate says “this is how I do it now” and it’s specific. I find those colleagues, record them, and get the recordings in front of their departments.

From the inside. not the vendor side.

I do change work inside large organisations myself. I spent years in change roles at a Belgian telco and work inside a Belgian insurer today, so I know what quarterly planning looks like when the AI workstream gets pushed to next quarter again. That’s the experience you’re buying.

Behaviour. not licences.

The number that matters is not seats activated. It’s how many people do one real task differently than they did in launch week. I count that at the start and again before I hand over, by asking people, not by reading a dashboard. If it isn’t moving, you hear it from me first.

It depends on what we agree on the call. The hand-over date is fixed at the start. I don’t do open-ended retainers.

Do you replace our change or L&D team? +

No. I work with them. Whatever we set up is theirs from day one. I build it with them and leave on the hand-over date.

Which AI tools do you work with? +

Whatever you’ve already bought. I’m not a reseller and I don’t recommend a switch unless the tool is the blocker, which it rarely is.
