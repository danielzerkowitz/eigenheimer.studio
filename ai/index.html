N° 02

The programme: what you get

    Three tools. One job.

  1. N° 01

    Adoption diagnosis

    Where usage stalls and why. Conversations with the people doing the work, not a survey. You get a written read-out you can put in front of your steering committee, with a baseline: who does what with the tool today. Usually where we start.

    2 to 3 wks
  2. N° 02

    Change programme

    The sequence, the roles, the rituals. Designed with your people, run by your people, with us alongside. Change management is the discipline; AI adoption is the case.

    per scope
  3. N° 03

    Facilitation

    Workshops and working sessions with the real work on the table. Group facilitation is the method: people change how they work in the room, not after the slides. Also bookable on its own: workshops.

    per session
  4. N° 04

    Peer conversations, recorded

    The studio arm. Early adopters describe what they actually do, in their own words, recorded as an internal podcast or video series. Machinery for overhearing.

    per episode
  5. N° 05

    Sparring

    A standing call with the programme lead, as often as it’s useful, for as long as we agreed. Someone to think with who has no stake in the tooling.

    ongoing
N° 03

Why this studio

N° 01

Adoption is overheard. not announced.

Nobody adopts a tool because the CEO’s video said so. They adopt it when a colleague they rate says “this is how I do it now” and it’s specific. We build the machinery that makes those conversations audible across the organisation.

N° 02

From the inside. not the vendor side.

We do change work inside large organisations ourselves. Daniel Zerkowitz runs this studio after years inside Belgian telco and, now, insurance, and knows what quarterly planning looks like when the AI workstream gets pushed to next quarter again. That’s the experience you’re buying.

N° 03

Behaviour. not licences.

The number that matters is not seats activated. It’s how many people do one real task differently than they did in launch week. We count that at the diagnosis and again before we hand over, by asking people, not by reading a dashboard. If it isn’t moving, you hear it from us first.

N° 05

Questions, before the call

How long is an engagement? +

Diagnosis is two to three weeks. A programme runs one to three quarters depending on scope, with a hand-over date fixed at the start. We don’t do open-ended retainers.

Do you replace our change or L&D team? +

No. We work with them. The programme is theirs from day one; our job is to make it work and then leave.

Which AI tools do you work with? +

Whatever you’ve already bought. We’re not a reseller and we don’t recommend a switch unless the tool is the actual blocker, which it rarely is.

Do you work in French? +

Sessions, conversations and recordings run in Dutch or English. French-speaking teams are served in English.

Can you sign our NDA and work through procurement? +

Yes. Standard NDA or yours, and a data processing agreement on request. Recordings and notes stay on your systems or are deleted after delivery. PO-based invoicing on your standard terms, Belgian VAT registered. We fit your supplier intake process.

What does it cost? +

Scoped on the call, quoted within five working days. Diagnosis is a fixed price; programme and facilitation are quoted per scope. All prices excl. btw.
