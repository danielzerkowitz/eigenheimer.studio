N° 03

Why this session

N° 01

Hands on keyboard. not slides.

Nobody learns a tool by watching someone else use it. Everyone in the room builds, and Daniel Zerkowitz is in the room with them, not a junior. If someone leaves without having typed anything, we’ve failed.

N° 02

Built for owner-managers. not developers.

No code you need to read, no jargon you need to nod at. The application you build is small on purpose: the point is that you built it and understand what it does.

N° 03

Two hours because that’s what it takes.

Not a two-day bootcamp. Not a lunch-and-learn. Two hours is long enough to build something and short enough that the whole management team actually turns up.

N° 04

Questions, before the session

Can the session run in Dutch? +

Yes. Dutch or English, whichever the room speaks.

What do participants need? +

A laptop and a paid seat on the tool we agree on beforehand. A free account is not enough for the build. We send a one-page setup guide; it takes ten minutes.

Which AI tool do you use? +

The one that fits your company: what you already have a licence for, or the one we’d recommend for a business your size. We’re not tied to any vendor.

What about our data? +

We pick tasks that need no customer personal data, and we work in business accounts that do not train on your input. If you already have a tool with a company policy, we work inside it.

How many people can join? +

Up to twelve. More than that and the build stops being hands-on. For a bigger group we run two sessions.

What does it cost? +

One fixed price per session, not per head, excl. btw, travel within Belgium included. The tool seats are yours. Ask and you’ll have a quote within one working day.
