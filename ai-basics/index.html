Who this is for

this is for you

  • You run a kmo, or a team inside a larger company, and you decide how people work.
  • You’ve used ChatGPT and suspect there’s more to it than a better search engine.
  • Your people ask “can AI do this?” and nobody has a good answer.
  • You want your team to leave with something built, not a slide deck.
  • You’d rather spend two hours than two days.

this isn’t for.

  • Companies looking for a strategy presentation.
  • Developers. You’re past this.
  • Teams that want a certificate.
  • Anyone who wants to watch a vendor demo. We work in the tool your team opens on Monday.

What you get

One session, a working tool per person
2h/1

Two hours, your office, your laptops. Your team goes from typing a question into a chat window to building a small tool around one of their own tasks: a quote drafted from an incoming email, a weekly report written from numbers you already have.

  1. Prompting that holds up

    How to get a reliable answer, how to know when not to trust one, how to hand it the retyping, summarising and first drafts.

    30 min

  2. Your own tasks

    A week before, you send us three tasks your people do every week. In the session, your people do them on the big screen, with Daniel next to them.

    30 min · 3 tasks

  3. Build

    From a prompt to a small working tool, by your people, in the room. Not a demo.

    45 min · 1 tool each

  4. Recap

    One page, within two working days: the tools you built, the next three tasks to hand to AI, ranked by your team in the room, and the ones to leave alone.

    15 min · 1 page

Why this session

Hands on keyboard. not slides.

Nobody learns a tool by watching someone else use it. Everyone in the room builds, and Daniel Zerkowitz is in the room with them, not a junior. If someone leaves without having typed anything, we’ve failed.

Built for owner-managers. not developers.

No code you need to read, no jargon you need to nod at. The tool you build is small on purpose: the point is that you built it and understand what it does.

Two hours because that’s what it takes.

Not a two-day training. Not a talk over sandwiches. Two hours is long enough to build something and short enough that the whole management team turns up.

Questions, before the session

Which language? +

Dutch or English, whichever the room speaks. French-speaking teams are served in English.

What do participants need? +

A laptop and a paid licence for the AI tool we agree on in the intake call. A free account is not enough for the build. We send a one-page setup guide; it takes ten minutes.

Which AI tool do you use? +

The one you already pay for: ChatGPT, Microsoft Copilot, Claude or Gemini. If you have none, we tell you on the intake call which one to take and why. No vendor pays us.

What about our data? +

We pick tasks that need no customer personal data, and we work in business accounts that do not train on your input. If you already have a tool with a company policy, we work inside it.

How many people can join? +

Up to twelve. More than that and the build stops being hands-on. For a bigger group we run two sessions.

What does it cost? +

One fixed price per session, the same for 4 or 12 people. Quote in writing within one working day. Excl. btw, travel within Belgium included. Licences for the AI tool are paid by you, not included.
