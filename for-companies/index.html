N° 05

Questions, before the call

Where does the audio live? +

Wherever you want it to. RSS feed for a podcast app, files for SharePoint or intranet, a feed your LMS can pull, or a public host like Spotify. Your call. We don’t keep copies after delivery - beyond what your contract permits.

Who owns the recordings and the IP? +

You. Full rights to the audio, the transcripts, the format. NDAs are standard. We can sign yours; we have one of our own if you’d rather use it.

What languages do you work in? +

Dutch and English. Most programmes run in the language people actually speak at work - we’ll match that, not the language that looks more polished on slides. French-speaking teams are served in English.

Do we need a recording room? +

A quiet meeting room is enough. We bring the gear, set up the acoustics, and break down before the next meeting. One hour per episode, including setup.

How does procurement / invoicing work? +

We invoice per episode or per block of episodes, on your standard terms, excl. btw. Belgian VAT registered. We can work against a PO, sign your NDA, and fit your supplier intake process. Recordings stay on your systems or are deleted after delivery.
